Brian & Figs

Bigger than two people have any right to sound

Myself and Fiachra (AKA Figs, Freddie Murphury, Father Fig) have been playing music together for over ten years. We’ve played in many bands together in the before times, but eventually decided to go out on our own as an acoustic duo. We've since played all across Ireland for weddings, Day 2s, engagement parties, birthdays and every other occasion you can think of. We're hugely passionate about music. Figs holds a degree in Jazz and a masters in sex appeal. I hold a guitar. Between us, it works. While all the instruments we use are a guitar, a bass and our two voices, we try songs that no one would expect a two piece to play. We're always looking to see how far we expand our repertoire and play the songs we all love. Are we brothers? Father and son? Best pals? Who knows.
