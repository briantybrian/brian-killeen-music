About me

I started playing guitar when I was 8 years old and have been endlessly obsessed with music ever since. Back then, all I wanted to be was the Angus Young type fella, just a man and his guitar speaking for him, but joining different bands, gigging across Ireland and playing for couples on their wedding days helped me find my actual voice and eventually became obsessed with songwriting too. Music has given me some incredible opportunities. I’ve had the pleasure of performing on radio and TV, I’ve done sound for other amazing musicians, performed for some amazing musicians too, given my voice to hundreds of songwriting projects all over the world and I get to perform for couples on their wedding day, which really is an honour. I love what I do and what it can do for others.
Whether you’re here to just listen, need music for your special day or need a voice for your song, music is the most important thing to me, and everything is treated as my own.
