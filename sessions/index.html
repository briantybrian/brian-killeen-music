Session Vocals

A voice for your project

I offer session vocals for songwriters, producers and bands who need a voice. I've been lending my voice to songwriters from all over the world since 2021. I treat every song as if it's my own and I'll chat with you about how you want the vocals to feel in order to give you my best performance. After I've done my part, I'll send you raw, dry vocal files that you can import to your project and freely mix. Happy to provide revisions if needed too.
