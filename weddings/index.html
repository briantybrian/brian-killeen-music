Weddings

Your Perfect Soundtrack

Wherever you say "I do", I can be there. I love getting to perform for couples on their wedding day and I'll work with you to create a setlist that's perfect for your day.

Ceremonies

Your ceremony set should be personal to you. Music that reflects the important moments in your lives together. I want to help capture that, so I'm happy to learn any songs I don't already know to make it happen. I've had the honour of performing at many ceremonies over the years, so if you need guidance on any part of it, I'm more than happy to help. And, if you like, I can also professionally record your ceremony songs so you have them to listen back to for years to come.

Receptions

Over years of gigging and performing, I've built up a setlist that spans every era, from niche gems to major hits, with something for every guest as they mingle and enjoy the afternoon. If there are songs that didn't quite make the ceremony cut, I'll play them during your reception drinks when you get back from having your photos done.

Booking in three steps

Get in touch

Send your date, venue and any song ideas using the form below and I'll come back to you with availability and a quote.

Plan the music

We talk through your playlist and ceremony programme, your favourites, where to put certain songs, or songs I can suggest for each part of the day.

On the day

I arrive early, well ahead of the ceremony or reception, to set up and make sure everything is perfect before your guests arrive. I bring backup cables and spare microphones too, so if the venue or your celebrant needs anything, I've got it covered.
